Machine learning allows computers to learn patterns from data. Supervised learning uses labeled examples to train models.

Artificial intelligence is used to solve complex problems such as image recognition, language understanding, and decision making.

Retrieval augmented generation combines document retrieval with language models to provide answers based on external knowledge.

Deep learning uses neural networks with multiple layers to learn complex patterns from large datasets.

Data preprocessing includes cleaning, transforming, and preparing data before it is given to a machine learning model.
